# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Tournament organizers, karate officials (referees, judges), and coaches. They operate in busy, brightly lit environments (like gymnasiums) and need to read data quickly under pressure.

## Product Purpose
A karate tournament operations console. It manages live scoring, brackets, athlete management, and tiesheet generation. Success means fast, error-free operation during a live event.

## Brand Personality
Flexible, Professional, Fast.

## Anti-references
- No glassmorphism.
- No "AI Slop" or generic AI-generated aesthetic templates.
- Avoid dark themes; the app must be strictly in light theme.
- Stick exclusively to the karate color palette: blue, white, red, black.

## Design Principles
- **Data over Decoration:** Visuals exist to organize data, not to decorate.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Every tap has a tactile, sub-200ms response.
- **High Contrast:** Must be readable in a bright gym.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA minimum contrast.
- Large tap targets (min 44px) for officials using tablets/phones.
- Reduced motion support.
